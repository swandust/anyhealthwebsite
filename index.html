FAQ

Answers your ops team will ask

What is AnyHealth.AI?+

AnyHealth.AI is a WhatsApp-first patient engagement and healthcare workflow automation platform. Clinics, hospital groups, dental, physio, chiropractic, aesthetic, TCM and ambulance operators get one AI assistant that books appointments 24/7, sends reminders, follows up after visits, runs targeted campaigns and coordinates non-emergency medical transport - with no app for patients to download.

Does it integrate with our existing CMS or EMR?+

Yes - that’s the point. AnyHealth sits on top of your existing CMS, CRM or EMR/EHR through secure API calling (HL7/FHIR-friendly). No workflow change, no new hardware, no migration. Reminders can be toggled on or off down to the individual patient level, and your team keeps the tools they already know.

How much does a WhatsApp chatbot reduce no-shows?+

In our pilot clinics, automated reminders with one-tap rescheduling brought no-show rates down from 28% to 10%, recovered roughly 180 of every 1,000 lapsed patients, and saved front-desk teams about 3 hours of manual messaging per day.

Can one bot really cover all our branches and services?+

Yes. One hospital group, one bot, every branch: patients share their location and AnyHealth finds the nearest outlet, answers branch-specific questions, and books across GP, dental, physio, chiro, aesthetics, TCM, specialist and ambulance services. One WhatsApp number can even serve outlets across Singapore, Malaysia and Hong Kong.

How long does implementation take?+

Days, not months. Because AnyHealth connects through APIs instead of replacing systems, there is no hardware to install and zero learning curve for staff. We co-create flows with your team, run a pilot, then scale branch by branch.

Where is patient data stored?+

All patient data remains at rest in ap-southeast-1 (Singapore), transported over a secure API gateway using industry-standard protocols. The AI operates with guarded context - it never gives medical advice outside its scope and escalates to your human team when it matters.
